About Me: Beyond the Interface

Some details are just
waiting to be noticed.

None of these were planned shoots. I just have a habit of noticing, a cat asleep in a stripe of light, a wall that's somehow making a face, a chip shaped like a boat. Small, beautiful, delightful things. The best design does the same thing, you feel it before you notice it, and noticing it is half the delight. That's the feeling I chase, whether I've got a camera in my hand or not.
