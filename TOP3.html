How the MoE Teaching Assistantship (TA) Stipend Works

1. Payout & Total Financial Value

  • Monthly Stipend: ₹12,400 per month paid directly to your account.
  • Duration: Paid for 24 months (2 full academic years).
  • Total Financial Payout: ₹2,97,600 total stipend received across the degree.
  • Financial Impact: Fully covers total tuition fees and living expenses at IITs and IISc.

2. Eligibility & Requirements

  • Qualified Institutions: Central Govt Technical Institutes only (IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IISc).
  • Entry Category: Must be admitted under the Regular / Teaching Assistantship (TA) category via CEED/GATE.
  • Weekly Duties: Must perform 8 to 10 hours per week of TA work (grading labs, helping professors, invigilating exams).
  • Academic Condition: Must maintain a minimum semester GPA (typically 6.0 / 10.0 or above).

3. Who does NOT get the Stipend?

  • NID Students: NIDs operate under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, not Ministry of Education. Zero MoE stipend provided.
  • Private Universities: MIT-ID Pune, Srishti, Symbiosis offer no MoE stipends.
  • Sponsored / Self-Financed Candidates: Candidates admitted under industry-sponsored seats at IITs do not qualify for TA stipends.
